Break-even ROAS at 70% margin: 1.43 (and 1.64 if revenue includes 15% VAT)

Last reviewed: 2026-10-29

Short answer: if 70% of each sale is left after product cost, shipping, payment fees and other per-order costs (but before ads), you break even at a ROAS of 1.43. Every 1 spent on ads must bring back 1.43 in net sales.

The formula

Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ contribution margin = 1 ÷ 0.70 = 1.43. Spend 10,000 on ads and you need 14,285.71 in net sales just to cover that spend. Contribution margin here means what's left from a sale before ad spend, as a share of the sale price excluding VAT.

Saudi 15% VAT: read your ads dashboard carefully

Meta, TikTok, Snap and Google usually report the purchase value your store sends, which on Salla, Zid and Shopify in Saudi Arabia often includes the 15% VAT. That VAT isn't yours, so the ROAS your dashboard shows must be at least 1.64 (1.43 × 1.15) to break even. If your pixel sends prices excluding VAT, use 1.43.

Target ROAS for a profit after ads

To keep a profit equal to a share of net sales after ads, ROAS = 1 ÷ (margin − target).

Worked example

You spend 10,000 SAR and the ads bring 30,000 SAR of net sales (ROAS 3.00). With a 70% margin you keep 21,000 SAR before ads, so the result is a profit of 11,000 SAR.
