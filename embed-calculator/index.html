Questions

Is the calculator free to embed?

Yes. You can embed it on any site, commercial or not. Please keep the small “Powered by Meezan” credit link visible.

Does the widget track my visitors?

No. It sets no cookies, loads no analytics or fonts, makes no network requests after loading, and sends no referrer. A strict Content-Security-Policy blocks all outside connections.

How is break-even ROAS calculated?

Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ contribution margin before ads. If your ads dashboard counts revenue including Saudi 15% VAT, multiply by 1.15.

How is net profit per order calculated?

Revenue ex-VAT (price ÷ 1.15 when the price includes VAT) minus product cost, shipping, the payment fee (fee % × the price the customer pays) and ad cost per order.

Can I show only one calculator or change the language?

Yes. Use calc=roas, calc=markup or calc=profit, and lang=ar or lang=en in the URL. Leave out calc to show all three with tabs.
