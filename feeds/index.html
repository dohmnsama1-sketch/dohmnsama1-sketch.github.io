Planned coverage: 2026-11-12 through 2028-12-31 (1939 dated items). After that, the generator re-issues earlier items with new ids so the feeds keep moving.

Before you connect: in Pinterest, claim this exact website: https://dohmnsama1-sketch.github.io. Auto-publish is under Settings → Create Pins in bulk → Auto-publish → Connect RSS feed, and is only offered on a business account with a claimed site. Every item link in these feeds is on that domain (Pinterest rejects off-domain links, including Gumroad).

How to connect (once)

  1. Open Pinterest on a desktop browser, signed in as abak1694.
  2. Claim https://dohmnsama1-sketch.github.io if it is not already claimed (Settings → Claimed accounts).
  3. Settings → Create Pins in bulk → Auto-publish → Connect RSS feed.
  4. Paste one feed URL, choose the matching board, Save. Repeat for each feed.
  5. Create Saudi Freelancer & VAT Tools and Large Print Puzzle Books first if they are still missing, then connect those two feeds. Until they exist, those Pins will not publish.

Pinterest creates Pins from new feed items within 24 hours, oldest first, up to 200 Pins per day across all feeds. This plan stays well under that cap.
