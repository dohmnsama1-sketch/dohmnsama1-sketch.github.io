Frequently asked questions

Are the books free?

No. The paperbacks are paid. The sample PDFs are free to print. Titles and pins say “sample” or “print” when they use the word Free.

What does 30-point type look like?

Print the sample and try it with a pencil. That is the only honest test; a screen preview is not the same as a printed grid.
