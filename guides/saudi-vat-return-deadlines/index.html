Saudi VAT return deadlines 2026–2027

Updated 12 Nov 2026. Not tax advice. Filing calendars can change — confirm due dates on zatca.gov.sa or in your ZATCA account before you file.

ZATCA has consistently described the due date as the last day of the month after the tax period. A ZATCA news item for the third quarter of 2026, for example, put that quarter’s deadline on 31 October 2026, which matches this rule. Use the official calendar if it ever differs.

Quarterly filers

Businesses and freelancers whose supplies are at or under SAR 40 million a year are generally quarterly filers (confirm your period in your ZATCA account). The four deadlines ahead:

PeriodTypical due date
Q4 2026 (1 Oct–31 Dec 2026)31 January 2027
Q1 2027 (1 Jan–31 Mar 2027)30 April 2027
Q2 2027 (1 Apr–30 Jun 2027)31 July 2027
Q3 2027 (1 Jul–30 Sep 2027)31 October 2027
Q4 2027 (1 Oct–31 Dec 2027)31 January 2028

Monthly filers

Above SAR 40 million in supplies, ZATCA generally requires monthly returns, due at the end of the following month — so January’s return is typically due 28 February 2027 (2027 is not a leap year), February’s on 31 March, and so on. Confirm in your account.

A spreadsheet is not a filing

You file on zatca.gov.sa. A workbook can help you gather sales and purchases and run pre-filing checks; it does not submit the return. If you are not yet registered and only want to watch the threshold, start with the free threshold monitor, not a return file.

Free: track your distance to the registration lines. Paid, if you already file: a return-prep workbook that rolls ledgers into return-style sections. Neither one files for you.

Frequently asked questions

What if the due date falls on a weekend or public holiday?

Follow ZATCA’s published rule for that year. Do not assume a spreadsheet or this page has moved the date for you.

Does Ramadan change the deadline?

Ramadan 1448 is expected around 8 February 2027, subject to official moon sighting. That sits inside Q1, whose typical deadline is 30 April 2027. Any official extension would be announced by ZATCA, not by this page.

المقدّمون شهريًا

فوق 40 مليون ريال في التوريدات، تطلب الهيئة عادة إقرارات شهرية، تُقدَّم في نهاية الشهر التالي — فإقرار يناير يحل عادة في 28 فبراير 2027 (2027 ليست سنة كبيسة)، وإقرار فبراير في 31 مارس، وهكذا. أكّد في حسابك.

الجدول ليس تقديمًا

التقديم على موقع الهيئة. يمكن لملف أن يجمع المبيعات والمشتريات ويجري فحوصات قبل التقديم؛ وهو لا يُرسل الإقرار. إن لم تكن مسجّلًا وتريد فقط مراقبة الحد، ابدأ بمراقب الحد المجاني، لا بملف الإقرار.

مجانًا: راقب مسافتك عن خطّي التسجيل. مدفوع، إن كنت تقدّم فعلًا: ملف تجهيز إقرار يلفّ الدفاتر إلى أقسام تشبه الإقرار. لا أحد منهما يقدّم عنك.
